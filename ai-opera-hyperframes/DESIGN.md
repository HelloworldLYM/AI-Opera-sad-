# AI-Opera HyperFrames Design

## Style Prompt

Dark Chinese opera technology stage: ink-black theatrical depth, gold cloud ornaments, restrained cyan HUD rings, slow cinematic camera moves, soft scan lines, deterministic particles, and bottom safe-area captions. The source posters remain intact; motion comes from camera transforms and transparent overlays.

## Colors

- Ink black: `#04070D`
- Opera gold: `#D7A84A`
- Warm text: `#F2E8D5`
- HUD cyan: `#20D7E8`
- Opera red: `#D92D20`

## Typography

- Chinese UI and captions: `Microsoft YaHei`, `SimHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, `PingFang SC`, sans-serif.
- Title fallback: `Microsoft YaHei`, `SimHei`, serif.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not alter, crop apart, OCR, rewrite, or regenerate the source poster text.
- Do not place effects over the main titles, major panels, or dense Chinese descriptions.
- Do not use fake Chinese text, software UI, editor UI, or code UI.
- Do not over-flash; motion should feel like a polished theatrical poster, not a glitch montage.
